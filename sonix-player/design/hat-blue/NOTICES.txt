The project's GPL-3.0 license is reproduced in LICENSE-upstream.txt. Retained utility SVGs and semantic quality badges originate from that repository and keep their original embedded notices. Their source copies are provided in this kit.

Their inclusion does not imply affiliation. Tidal's four-diamond layout follows the existing repository artwork. Qobuz uses a simplified Q/record construction matching the established identity.
